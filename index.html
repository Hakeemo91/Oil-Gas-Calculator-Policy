Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Oil & Gas Calculator (also known as SWT Calculator, "the App") is a utility tool for oil and gas field calculations, available on iOS, Android and Huawei AppGallery. This Privacy Policy explains what the App does and does not do with information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We have no user accounts. All calculations run locally on your device. The values you enter, your results, your favourites and your saved wells stay on your device, and we never receive them.

2. Advertising

The App shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain data from your device, such as your advertising identifier, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, crash and performance information, and how you interact with ads. This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Your Choices

  • iOS: the App asks your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you may be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

4. Permissions

The App needs internet access so that ads can load. It does not request access to your contacts, microphone, precise location or other sensitive data. If you choose to import a survey file or photo in the N2 Underbalance calculator, the file is read on your device and is not uploaded to us.

5. Other Third-Party Services

Apart from Google AdMob, the App does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for oil and gas professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We hold no personal data about you. Data saved inside the App lives only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
